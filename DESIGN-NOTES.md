# Editorial preferences

Do not add decorative numbered eyebrow labels such as “01 / OPEN FESTIVAL”. Keep meaningful dates, navigation filters and genuine ordered instructions.
Activity cards use a clear activity name, photo and corresponding approved ribbon illustration beside the title. Avoid slogans, caption filler and category badges on photos. Classification belongs in catalogue filters.
