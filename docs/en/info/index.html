QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good question

Is entry really free?+

Yes. Entry to the festival and market is free on 23 January. Cup entry, rentals, sauna, food and individual services may cost extra.

Do I need a team to visit?+

No. You need a team for Winter Cup. Open activities, the market and music are for general visitors too.

What happens on 22 January?+

22 January is for Winter Cup team competitions, with spectators welcome. The market, public skating, sauna village and Winter After Dark concert take place on 23 January.

Can I bring children?+

Yes, family activities are part of the plan. Individual age requirements are being confirmed. Children attend with an accompanying adult.

Can I bring my own equipment?+

Requirements for personal skis and skates will be published on the activity pages. Check them alongside rental rules before travelling.

Do I need to book ahead?+

Not for general festival entry. Teams register through Fienta. Booking for the ski event, rink and sauna is being confirmed separately.

What if the weather changes?+

Parts of the sports programme depend on snow, ice and wind. Updates will appear in the programme and activity pages; check before setting off.

Where is the map and exact entrance?+

The exact entrance, parking and site map will be added to the visit section once approved. The current location is the Narva-Jõesuu seafront and Suur-Lootsi area.
