Data & privacy

How site features relate to your data and who to contact.

Language & preferences

The site remembers your chosen language in your device’s browser. This is not a subscription or registration. We have not added advertising trackers or analytics systems to the site code.

Registration & external pages

Teams continue to Fienta, while traders and volunteers use external forms. Read the service terms and form notices before sending data. This site itself does not take payments or store applications.

Messages to the organiser

Email links open your mail application. A message is sent only when you send it. Contact the organiser about correspondence or data at info@winterfest.ee.

Hosting platform

The site is hosted on ChatGPT Sites. The platform may process technical data to provide access, security and site operation under its own terms.
