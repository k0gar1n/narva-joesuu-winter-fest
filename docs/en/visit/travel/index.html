Three ways to arrive. One winter shore.

23.01.2027
Narva-Jõesuu

01

By car

Head for the winter coast

From Tallinn, head towards Narva and then Narva-Jõesuu. The town guide estimates around three hours; allow extra time for winter weather and road conditions.

Festival parking arrangements will be published separately. Keep the beach and service access clear. The town map currently shows the destination, not the festival entrance.

Route to the town↗
02

By bus

Via Narva to the coast

Take an intercity bus to Narva, then a local bus to Narva-Jõesuu. Check connections for your travel date.

Check the transfer and return journey before buying tickets. Winter and holiday timetables may vary; no dedicated festival shuttle has been announced.

Intercity buses↗Local bus routes↗
03

By train

Take the train to Narva

Trains from Tallinn arrive in Narva. Continue to Narva-Jõesuu by local bus or taxi. Narva-Jõesuu does not have its own railway station.

If bringing skis or other large equipment, check the operator’s baggage rules and allow time for your transfer.

Elron timetable↗Bus from Narva↗

The last part of the journey

The festival is planned for the seafront area of Narva-Jõesuu: Suur-Lootsi cultural quarter, surrounding grounds and beach. The exact entrance, parking and site map will be published once agreed.
