One day
Plenty to remember

Winter Cup brings team games to the winter coast of Narva-Jõesuu. Get five friends, relatives or acquaintances together for a day of challenges, saunas and live music.

No sporting experience needed. Some games call for accuracy, others for speed, teamwork or a little encouragement. Aim for first place or enjoy trying everything together.

The whole team together

From challenges to the evening concert

Meet in the morning, hear the briefing and head to the game stations. You stay together, collecting points towards your team result. There is time to eat and rest between games, then enjoy the saunas, awards and participants’ concert. There will be small prizes for the winners.

All included in the team fee

Who to bring

Friends, family, neighbours or acquaintances: five people aged 16 or over. You do not need to work or study together, live in the same town or belong to a sports club.

What challenges await you

These are stations within one team competition. You complete each station together and build a shared overall score

Places are limited

Get your friends together

Choose a team ticket

Enter your team name and a contact person to receive the event information. You can provide the full roster later.

Confirm your place with payment

Your fee is fixed on the application date. Payment secures your team’s place. Games, equipment, two meals, saunas and the evening programme are included.

Send your roster by 19 January

Email the five participants’ names to info@winterfest.ee. We will send your team schedule and meeting details. Bring warm clothes and a change of clothing; arrange your own travel.

Short of a full team?

Come on your own or as a group of two, three or four — we will find the rest of your team. Buy one ticket per person in the same order to stay together. Entry costs €20, €25 or €30 per person, depending on when you register.
