Who may participate?+

Teams are formed, entered and paid for by a city or rural municipal government. Eligible participants are its officials and employees, employees of institutions or enterprises under its authority or ownership, and members of its city or rural municipal council. Eligible organisations include municipal schools, kindergartens, libraries, cultural, sporting and social institutions, and municipal enterprises. Living in the municipality or receiving funding from its budget does not, on its own, establish eligibility.

Team size and number of teams+

Each team has five participants aged 18 or over. No reserve participants are registered. There are no restrictions on sporting ability or competitive level. A municipality may enter multiple teams subject to availability. Each team registers, pays and competes separately. Joint teams representing different municipalities are not permitted.

Flags and municipal parade+

The programme includes a parade of municipalities. Teams are encouraged to bring their municipality’s flag or other symbols. The sporting programme is followed by rest, festival activities, a show, the award ceremony and a concert for participants.

Food+

Travel, parking and team buses+

The event takes place in Narva-Jõesuu. Drop-off, bus parking, the entrance and venue map are being confirmed.Getting here↗

Clothing, equipment and changing+

The fee includes competition entry, necessary equipment, two meals for the five participants, access to saunas and activities included in the programme, heated changing rooms, storage facilities, the participants’ show programme, award ceremony and concert. The municipality arranges and pays for its own transport. Participants should bring warm outdoor clothing and a change of clothes. Meals for accompanying guests are not included in the team package.

Accessibility and on-site help+

Contact the organiser ahead of time about any access requirements. Accessible routes, on-site support and weather contingency information will be included in the participant guide.

Accommodation and supporters+

The municipality arranges transport and accommodation separately. Meals for accompanying guests are not included in the team fee.

Roster and replacements+

By 19 January 2027, the coordinator must email info@winterfest.ee with the five participants’ first and last names and workplaces. For council members, specify the relevant council. The coordinator confirms that the roster is accurate and the participants are eligible. Employment contracts and other supporting documents are not required. If a participant cannot attend, another eligible participant may replace them. Notify info@winterfest.ee, identifying the team, the participant being replaced, and the new participant’s name and workplace. The team remains five people.

Results and ties+

Each challenge is assessed on a ten-point scale. Scoring criteria are set out in the relevant discipline’s rules. A team’s scores are added together; the team with the highest total wins. All municipal teams compete in one ranking, without divisions based on population or other characteristics of their city or municipality. There is no separate final. Teams placing first, second and third receive awards. If totals are equal, the number of scores of 10 is compared, then scores of 9 and so on in descending order. If all counts are equal, the teams share the placing. A challenge missed by a team scores 0. A challenge cancelled by the organiser is excluded from every team’s total.

Result queries+

The team captain submits any queries about results to the head official. Queries are considered before results are finalised and awards presented. There is no fee for submitting a query. Queries must be submitted within 30 minutes of the publication of provisional results, before the award ceremony.

Cancellation, postponement and withdrawal+

Weather or other circumstances may require the organiser to change the programme, cancel individual activities, postpone or cancel the event. Changes will be communicated to team coordinators and published on the website. If the event is cancelled, the organiser refunds the full entry fee paid. If it is postponed, the municipality may choose to attend on the new date or receive a full refund. Contact info@winterfest.ee about refunds. If a team withdraws after payment, its entry fee is not refunded. Participants may be replaced as described in section 3.
