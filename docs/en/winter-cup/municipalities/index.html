One day
One team

Winter Cup brings city and rural municipal teams together in competition. Five colleagues represent their municipality in winter sports and team challenges.

Swap the working day for a shared route, new roles and supporting each other. Meet colleagues from other institutions and municipalities, join the parade under your flag and compete together for a place on the podium.

The whole team together

From challenges to the evening concert

After check-in and briefing, your team follows its own schedule between stations, earning points towards the overall result. There are meal and rest breaks, followed by saunas, a show, awards for the top three teams and a participants’ concert.

All included in the team fee

Who can join your team

Municipal government staff, employees of its subordinate institutions and municipally owned enterprises, and council members. Each team has five participants aged 16+, with no sporting-level requirement. A municipality may enter several teams.

What challenges await you

These are stations within one team competition. You complete each station together and build a shared overall score

For the municipal coordinator

Apply on behalf of your municipality

Enter the municipality, team name, coordinator’s contact and billing details in Fienta. The full roster is not needed yet.

Pay the invoice

The organiser will issue an invoice using the details provided. Payment confirms your team’s place. The fee is fixed on the application date.

Send the roster by 19 January

Email the five participants’ names and workplaces to info@winterfest.ee. The coordinator will receive the team’s schedule and arrival information.
