Winter Cup brings municipal teams together for sporting challenges on the winter coast of Narva-Jõesuu. The shared result comes from the whole team’s effort.

Who can represent a municipality?+

This category is for cities and municipalities. The required connection — employment, residence or another criterion — will be defined in the general rules.

How many people and teams may enter?+

Team size, substitutes, age requirements and the number of teams per municipality are being confirmed. Finalise your roster once these conditions are published.

How can we represent our municipality?+

Consider a shared team name, colours and a flag. Arrangements for municipal introductions, use of symbols and the award ceremony will be published in the programme.

Food+

Included in the entry fee. Menu, portions, serving times and dietary arrangements will be published here.

Travel, parking and team buses+

The event takes place in Narva-Jõesuu. Drop-off, bus parking, the entrance and venue map are being confirmed.Getting here↗

Clothing, equipment and changing+

Plan clothing for an active outdoor day, a warm layer for breaks and a change of clothes. Supplied equipment, changing facilities and storage are being confirmed.

Accessibility and on-site help+

Contact the organiser ahead of time about any access requirements. Accessible routes, on-site support and weather contingency information will be included in the participant guide.

Accommodation and supporters+

Arrange accommodation and travel separately. Supporters are welcome: entry to the general festival site is free. Catering arrangements for accompanying guests are being confirmed.Where to stay↗
