Winter Run participation rules · 23 January 2027

1. Venue and organiser+

Winter Run takes place on 23 January 2027 in Narva-Jõesuu as part of Estonia Winter Fest. Start and finish at the harbour, Suur-Lootsi 4. Organiser: MTÜ Noorteaeg. Contact: info@winterfest.ee, +372 5557 2817.

2. Distances+

3.5 km — one lap; 7 km — two laps; children’s run — 300 m. The main course follows Aia street and the Vabaduse pedestrian path. Choose your distance when registering.

3. Registration and fees+

Register through Fienta or on site during bib collection. Until 31 December 2026: 3.5 km and 7 km — €10; children’s run — €5. From 1 January 2027 and on the day: 3.5 km and 7 km — €15; children’s run — €8. There is no participant limit.

4. Schedule+

Bib collection 11:00–12:00. Children’s start 12:00, 7 km start 12:30, 3.5 km start 12:40. Awards are planned for approximately 13:30. The awards location will be announced separately.

5. Timing and awards+

Electronic timing is provided for 3.5 km and 7 km. The top three men and women in each distance receive awards; there are no separate age-group rankings. The children’s run has no rankings. Every finisher receives a medal.

6. Included with entry+

Your selected race, a race bib, electronic timing for 3.5 km and 7 km, and a finisher medal. One admission to the sauna area between 13:45 and 15:00 with your bib. A place for belongings, a changing room and a shower are available. Bring your own sauna essentials. Festival and concert admission is free.

7. On the course+

Choose a distance suitable for your fitness and follow course markings and organisers’ instructions. The winter course may be slippery. Wear suitable clothes and footwear. Tell the organisers if you withdraw from the race.

8. Changes and cancellation+

The organiser may postpone or cancel the race due to bad weather. In the event of cancellation, entry fees are refunded in full. Changes will be published on the website and sent to registered participants. If postponed, the new date and next steps will be communicated separately.
