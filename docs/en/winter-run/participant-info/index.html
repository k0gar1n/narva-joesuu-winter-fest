WINTER RUN · 23.01.2027

Participant info

Register↗

What you need before the start and after the finish.

3.5 and 7 km10 €
Children’s run5 €

Online until 31 December 2026: €10 / children €5. From 1 January 2027 and on the day: €15 / children €8.

Bibs and registration+

Collect your bib on 23 January between 11:00 and 12:00 at the harbour, Suur-Lootsi 4. Have your Fienta confirmation ready. When buying multiple tickets, enter the details of each participant separately. On-site registration is available during bib collection.

Distance and results+

3.5 km is one lap; 7 km is two laps. Both distances have electronic timing. The 300 m children’s run has no rankings. A results link will be published here after the race.

What to bring+

Winter running clothes, gloves, a hat and shoes with good grip. Bring dry, warm clothes to change into after the finish. For the saunas, bring your own towel, swimwear and slippers.

Bags, changing and showers+

The festival has a place to leave your belongings, a changing room, a shower and toilets. Locations will be included in the pre-race information.

Saunas after the race+

Between 13:45 and 15:00, runners can enter the sauna area once for free by showing their race bib. Keep your bib after finishing. Bring your own sauna essentials.

Medals and awards+

Every finisher receives a medal. The top three men and women are awarded separately in the 3.5 km and 7 km races. The children’s run has no rankings. Awards are planned for approximately 13:30; the location will be confirmed.

Weather and updates+

Check this page before travelling. The organiser may postpone or cancel the race in unsafe weather. If the race is cancelled, entry fees are refunded in full. Registered participants will be notified of changes.
