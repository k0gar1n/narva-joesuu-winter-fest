The festival
at a glance

NARVA-JÕESUU WINTER FEST is a winter festival of sport, music and gathering by the sea, taking place on 22–23 January 2027 in Narva-Jõesuu. Open activities run on both days, with the team-based Winter Cup planned for 23 January. Visitor entry is free.
