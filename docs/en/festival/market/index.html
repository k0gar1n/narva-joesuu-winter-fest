23.01.2027 · NARVA-JÕESUU

Winter market

Seaside market stalls, strings of lights and hot mulled wine. Freshly cooked food, local produce and winter gifts at Estonia Winter Fest.

23 January 202711:00–20:00 · Narva-Jõesuu

Free entry

At the market

Mulled wine and hot drinks+

Hot mulled wine, tea and coffee. Take a drink along as you browse the stalls, or stop for a moment to warm up.

Street food and local produce+

Food trucks, dishes cooked on site and goods from local producers. Eat at the festival and pick up something to take home.

Gifts and winter decorations+

Handmade goods, souvenirs, decorations and little things for your home. Take a closer look and meet their makers.

Visiting the market

Where is the market?+

In Narva-Jõesuu, at the Suur-Lootsi cultural quarter and the surrounding area, in the heart of the festival.

Do I need a ticket?+

Entry to the market is free. Food, drinks and purchases are paid for separately at the stalls.

Can I come just for the market?+

Yes. No registration or participation in the competitions is needed. The market is open on 23 January, 11:00–20:00.
