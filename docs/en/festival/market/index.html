WINTER MARKET

A taste of winter
Right by the sea

Between sports, make time for something hot. The winter market brings together street food, local produce and handmade finds.

Images illustrate the atmosphere of the planned event.

IN THIS AREA

What to expect

01

Something hot

Food trucks, street food and hot drinks are planned. Traders and menus will be announced closer to the festival.

02

Made nearby

Local businesses, craftspeople and producers are central to the market. Meet the people making things in our region.

03

Both days

The market is part of the open festival on 23 and 23 January. You do not need a team or Cup entry to visit.

When
22–23 January 2027
Where
Narva-Jõesuu
The coastal festival area

Market entry is free. Food, drinks and products are purchased separately.
