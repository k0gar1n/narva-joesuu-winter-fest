First time on skis?
Start here

Try cross-country skiing at Estonia Winter Fest. These lessons are for complete beginners and anyone with a little experience who wants to feel more confident on skis.

Everything for your first ski lesson

Skis, poles and boots are provided on site. All you need to bring is warm clothing.

In Estonian and Russian

Instruction is available in both languages.

No experience needed

Join us if you have never skied before or have only tried it a few times.

How to sign up

Lessons are free, but advance registration is required. The form link will appear here.

Registration opens soon

Submitting the form does not guarantee a place. The organiser will confirm your participation by email.
