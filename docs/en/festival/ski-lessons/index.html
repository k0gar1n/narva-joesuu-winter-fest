Free ski lessons

23 January 2027

For beginners · ≈ 45 minutes · 25 people per group

Register

No experience needed

Suitable even if you have never skied before.

Equipment included

Skis, poles and boots are provided on site.

Estonian and Russian

Instruction is available in both languages.

Bring warm clothing

You do not need to bring your own ski equipment.
