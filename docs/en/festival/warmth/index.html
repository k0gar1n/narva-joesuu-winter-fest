WARM UP & UNWIND

Sauna Villages

A winter festival needs room for both movement and rest. Saunas, warm-up areas and places to pause are planned between activities.

IN THIS AREA

What to expect

01

Sauna

A separate service with its own participation terms. Sessions, capacity and prices will follow once agreed with the operator.

02

Warm-up areas

Rest and changing areas form part of site planning. Locations will be shown on the approved festival map.

03

A fire & hot drinks

A gathering place by the fire and hot drinks at the market are planned. Exact arrangements are being confirmed.

When
23 January 2027
Where
Narva-Jõesuu
The coastal festival area

Sauna access is not automatically included in free festival entry.
