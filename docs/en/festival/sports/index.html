IN THIS AREA

What to expect

01

Football on snow

A team game with a winter twist, plus accuracy challenges. Open activities and Cup tasks will have different formats.

02

Volleyball

Serves, passes and playing together. Court details, rules and team sizes are being confirmed.

03

Biathlon

A combination of movement and accuracy. Equipment and the introductory format will be announced with the instructor.

04

Curling

Try sending the stone towards the target and getting a feel for the tactics. The playing surface is being confirmed.

05

Ice hockey

Get to know the stick, puck and movement on ice. The programme depends on rink preparation.
