# Winter Cup for everyone
General rules · Estonia Winter Fest 2027
Version 1.0 · 22 September 2026

## 1. Date, location and organiser

The competition takes place on 22 January 2027 in Narva-Jõesuu as part of Estonia Winter Fest. The organiser is MTÜ Noorteaeg. For participation enquiries, contact info@winterfest.ee.

The programme runs from 12:00 to 16:00. All teams share the same start and finish times. Each team receives its own schedule for challenges, meals and rest. Exact times, the meeting point and the route will be sent to the designated coordinator.

## 2. Eligibility

Teams of friends, relatives, acquaintances and club members are welcome. You do not need to represent a company or municipality.

No sporting experience is required: the challenges suit participants with different levels of experience.

Each team has five participants aged 16 or over. No reserve participants are registered. There are no restrictions on sporting ability or competitive level.

Multiple teams may register subject to availability. Each team registers, pays and competes separately.

## 3. Registration, roster and replacements

Registration and payment take place through Fienta. Enter your team name and a contact person. You can provide the full roster later. Payment during registration secures your place. Places are limited.

By 19 January 2027, the coordinator must email info@winterfest.ee with the five participants’ first and last names.

If a participant cannot attend, another participant aged 16 or over may replace them. Email info@winterfest.ee with the team name, the participant being replaced and the new participant’s name. The team remains five people.

## 4. Entry fee

Prices are final and apply to a team of five.

| Registration order | Price |
| --- | ---: |
| First 20 registered teams — sold out | €75 |
| Next 20 teams (21–40) | €100 |
| Next 20 teams (41–60) | €150 |

The fee depends on the team’s registration order and is fixed when the application is submitted through Fienta. Payment is made during registration in Fienta.

The fee includes competition entry, necessary equipment, a hot lunch for the whole team, access to saunas and activities included in the programme, heated changing rooms, storage facilities, the participants’ show programme, award ceremony and concert. The team arranges and pays for its own transport.

Participants should bring warm outdoor clothing and a change of clothes. Meals for accompanying guests are not included in the team package.

## 5. Programme and challenges

The team completes challenges together, moving between stations according to its own schedule. Meal and rest times are staggered and may differ between teams.

The list of challenges, their descriptions, procedures, safety requirements and scoring rules are published separately on the category page. These general rules establish the conditions of participation; each discipline’s rules specify how that challenge is conducted.

The programme includes a team parade. Bring your team’s flag or other team colours if you wish. The sporting programme is followed by rest, festival activities, a show, the award ceremony and a concert for participants.

## 6. Results and awards

Each challenge is assessed on a ten-point scale. Scoring criteria are set out in the relevant discipline’s rules. A team’s scores are added together; the team with the highest total wins.

Teams in the open category compete in their own separate ranking. There is no separate final. Teams placing first, second and third receive awards.

If totals are equal, the number of scores of 10 is compared, then scores of 9 and so on in descending order. If all counts are equal, the teams share the placing. A challenge missed by a team scores 0. A challenge cancelled by the organiser is excluded from every team’s total.

## 7. Officials and safety

Participants must follow discipline rules and instructions from officials, instructors, medical personnel and security staff. The organiser provides the necessary equipment, medical support and event security. Participants must take account of their health and seek assistance from on-site medical personnel when needed.

The team captain submits any queries about results to the head official. Queries are considered before results are finalised and awards presented. There is no fee for submitting a query.

Queries must be submitted within 30 minutes of the publication of provisional results, before the award ceremony.

## 8. Changes, postponement and cancellation

Weather or other circumstances may require the organiser to change the programme, cancel individual activities, postpone or cancel the event. Changes will be communicated to team coordinators and published on the website.

If the event is cancelled, the organiser refunds the full entry fee paid. If it is postponed, the team may choose to attend on the new date or receive a full refund. Contact info@winterfest.ee about refunds.

If bad weather or other unforeseen circumstances prevent a team from taking part, the organiser refunds the full amount paid. Participants may also be replaced as described in section 3.

## 9. Contact and team communication

The coordinator receives event information and shares the daily schedule, general rules and discipline rules with participants. Send rosters and roster changes to info@winterfest.ee. Use the same address for questions about registration, payment, food and travel arrangements.
